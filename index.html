Who Am I?

About Me

I am Abu Sayeem Rafi, an undergraduate Computer Science and Engineering student at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT).

I'm from Rajshahi, Bangladesh.

I like the acronym of my name, ASR, and use it as my nickname across all my digital creations. I deeply admire creative works—both digital and handmade—while equally enjoying logical reasoning and problem-solving.

I am particularly drawn to story-based games and interactive experiences, and my career goal is to to join academia and additionally to work in and contribute to the game industry.
